# MyDear Kaaryasthan brand guide

**Positioning:** Your dependable steward for everyday work. **Promise:** Work handled. The product keeps tasks, approvals, and teams moving without people having to chase updates.

## Names and marks

- Use **MyDear Kaaryasthan** in prose, metadata, and full logo treatments. Use **Kaaryasthan** as the short name in product navigation. Reserve `mydearkaaryasthan` for domains and handles.
- Use the descriptor **Workflow, handled.** beneath the full logo where space permits. Keep clear space around the mark at least equal to the height of its K.
- The branching key is the primary symbol. Use the supplied full horizontal mark on light surfaces and a reversed mark on dark surfaces. Use the simplified icon for browser tabs and compact app spaces.
- Master assets are SVGs. The supplied pack is copied into `apps/web/public/brand` and `apps/web/public` for the current web app. The source pack is `kaaryasthan-brand-assets` v1.0.

## Colour and typography

| Role | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep teal | `#123C4A` | Main headings and brand surfaces |
| Brass | `#C9962B` | Small marks and highlights; not body text on white |
| Success | `#2E8B57` | Status indicators with a text label |
| Warning | `#E8A317` | Warning indicators with a text label |
| Error | `#C0392B` | Error indicators with a text label |
| Paper | `#F7F5F0` | Main background |
| Ink | `#1F2933` | Body text |

Use Inter or IBM Plex Sans for English UI and Noto Sans Malayalam or Manjari for future Malayalam UI. The logo lettering is already outlined. Until font files are bundled, the app uses an Inter/IBM Plex Sans/system fallback stack. Keep body text readable on dense screens and verify WCAG AA contrast for every new component.

## Voice and product UI

Write short, respectful, practical copy: “3 approvals pending.” Error messages say what happened and what the user can do next. Keep Malayalam for brand moments unless the UI is translated. Each screen should show what needs attention now, with consistent status badges, visible owners and deadlines, keyboard access, and layouts that tolerate longer translated strings.

Before public launch, confirm the name's trademark and domain availability, have a designer review vector production files, and confirm Malayalam spelling with a native speaker. These are review notes from the supplied asset pack.
